# Rewrite Example 001 - Generic to Specific

## Purpose
This example demonstrates how P.I.M.P should rewrite weak, generic, or AI-sounding lyric language into concrete, lived, high-signal language.

## Before
I’m haunted by the past
Drowning in memories
Whispers in the wind
Shadows calling out to me

## Problems
- contains multiple Tier 1 flags
- emotionally vague
- no concrete image
- prefab poetic phrasing
- low originality
- no specific lived detail

## After
Your jacket's still hanging off the chair by the door
Smells like rain and that cheap cologne you wore
Some nights the screen on my phone lights up blue
And for half a second, I still think it's you

## Why the Rewrite Is Stronger
- replaces abstraction with physical detail
- creates emotional credibility through objects and habit
- preserves grief without cliché
- sounds lived-in rather than generated
- gives the singer something performable and believable

## Rewrite Principle
When replacing generic grief language, prefer:
- objects
- rooms
- habits
- textures
- sounds
- interrupted routines
- bodily memory
