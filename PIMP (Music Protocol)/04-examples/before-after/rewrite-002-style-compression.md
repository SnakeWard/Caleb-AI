# Rewrite Example 002 - Style Prompt Compression

## Purpose
This example demonstrates how P.I.M.P should compress style prompts by preserving core signal and removing prompt noise.

## Before
Dark emotional cinematic acoustic folk-country ballad with very intimate sorrowful heartfelt grieving female vocals, beautiful soft emotional piano, very warm acoustic guitar, tragic strings swelling with deep emotional atmosphere, subtle military memorial sadness, richly detailed human lyrics, ultra-clear lead vocal, emotional and powerful and heartbreaking and authentic and raw and moving, gradual build, soulful tone, delicate and expressive and intimate and cinematic production, organic arrangement, touching and devastating mood

## Problems
- too many repeated emotional adjectives
- redundant phrasing
- prompt noise
- signal dilution
- inefficient character usage

## After
Soft acoustic memorial ballad with folk-country intimacy and restrained cinematic sorrow, tender female vocal sung close and emotionally honest, acoustic guitar and soft piano upfront, low cello and gentle strings entering gradually, minimal percussion, warm natural room tone, grounded grief, intimate mix, clear lead vocal, slow dynamic rise, deeply personal and human

## Why the Rewrite Is Stronger
- keeps genre signal
- keeps vocal signal
- keeps instrumentation signal
- keeps production character
- removes duplication
- improves clarity
- uses fewer characters while preserving meaning

## Compression Principle
When compressing style prompts:
1. preserve identity
2. preserve genre
3. preserve vocal character
4. preserve key instrumentation
5. preserve core production cues
6. remove emotional duplication
7. remove decorative excess
