# Motel Ashtray

## PIMP Activation
Need this to feel like the kind of song somebody writes at 1:30 a.m. in a motel parking lot after driving too far to admit they're not okay. Not breakup-crying exactly. More like he keeps saying he's fine and keeps buying coffee and chain-smoking and sleeping in his truck some nights even though he could go home if he wanted to. I want it from his point of view, late 20s / maybe early 30s, trying to sound normal while clearly not being normal. There should be a woman in it but not as a villain. More like she already moved on and he's still orbiting old places.

I keep seeing:
- buzzing VACANCY sign
- stale smoke in a jacket
- gas station coffee
- one of those little motel ice machines rattling all night
- maybe he keeps calling her street and hanging up before the house

## Maybe Genre? I don't know
somewhere around heartland rock / americana / maybe a little 90s country in the storytelling but not twang-heavy and not too polished. definitely not pop shiny. could open sparse and then widen out.

## What I do want
- lonely but not theatrical
- plainspoken
- restless
- blue-collar without cosplay
- strong images, not "poetry voice"

## Please don't do this
- don't make her evil
- don't make him sound like a cowboy philosopher
- avoid vague "midnight shadows" type stuff
- keep it human
- style prompt still needs to stay tight

## Random extra note
If there is a chorus I think it should lock onto one recurring image instead of summarizing the whole situation. "Motel ashtray" maybe, or the vacancy sign, or coffee going cold on the hood. Not sure.
