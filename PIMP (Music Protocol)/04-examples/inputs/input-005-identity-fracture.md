# PIMP Activation

Song called "Good Man, Mostly"

This is a guy talking like he's decent, like he’s got his life together, but the more he talks the more it’s clear he’s cut corners, lied, maybe cheated, and just keeps justifying it.

He never fully admits it. He keeps reframing everything so he still sounds like a good guy.

## What I do want
- subtle self-deception
- not dramatic
- almost conversational
- you realize the truth before he does

## Maybe Genre? I don't know
heartland rock, maybe a little Springsteen-esque storytelling but stripped down

## Random notes
- he uses phrases like "I did what I had to"
- mentions small things like late paychecks, missed calls, stuff like that
- no big crime, just accumulated compromise

## Please don't do this
- no big confession moment
- no villain monologue
- no dramatic twist ending
