# PIMP Activation

Song called "Break Room Smile"

Factory or warehouse job. Guy everyone likes, always joking, always smiling, but he's drowning in debt and pressure at home.

Nobody knows. He keeps it locked down. Every day he shows up and plays the role.

- contrast between public persona and private reality
- not sad on the surface
- almost upbeat delivery but wrong underneath

upbeat heartland rock or light country rock, something that could almost pass as feel-good if you don’t listen closely

- he laughs a second too long

## Please don't do this
