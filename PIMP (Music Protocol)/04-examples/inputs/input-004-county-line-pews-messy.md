# County Line Pews

## PIMP thing
Need a song that feels like a man sitting in a church he has no business feeling comfortable in. Not anti-faith exactly. More like he still believes enough for it to hurt. He helped cover for his younger brother after a wreck, or at least kept his mouth shut when he should have said what really happened. Nobody died but somebody got messed up bad enough that he keeps seeing it when the room gets quiet. I want the singer older than the brother, maybe early 40s, blue-collar, not dramatic, somebody who does practical things with his hands and is now stuck with something he can't fix with his hands.

Stuff I keep seeing:
- folded bulletin in his fist
- hymn he can't quite sing
- mud still dried on work boots
- county line / two-lane / ditch water / sheriff lights way back in memory
- communion tray coming closer and he lets it pass

## Sort of where it lives
americana maybe, maybe heartland-adjacent, maybe a little southern gospel shadow in the harmony feel but not choir-cheesy. I don't want it polished. It should feel worn down, steady, and a little ashamed.

## What I mean by it
- restrained
- guilty
- tired
- plainspoken
- morally cornered

## Please don't screw this up
- don't turn it into a murder ballad
- don't make it revengey
- don't make him sound like a poet-preacher
- don't make the brother cartoon evil either
- keep the confession partial until late if possible

## extra
If there is a chorus I think it should lock onto one church object or ritual instead of explaining the whole backstory. Bulletin, pew, hymn, communion tray, something like that.
