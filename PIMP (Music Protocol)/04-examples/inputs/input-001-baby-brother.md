# Example Input 001 - Baby Brother

## Title
Baby Brother

## Raw User Intent
PIMP activate on a sad soft song called “Baby Brother” sung from a sister’s perspective. She is a few years older. The song starts in infancy and talks about fits and fights, as well as giggles and games of youth. Then it turns to teenage years and the girls he dates and the speeding tickets and how much trouble he will get. Then the song turns to baby brother serving overseas, and the tragic welcome home comes in the form of a wooden box and a 21-gun salute goodbye.

## Desired Direction
- emotionally devastating
- grounded
- human
- not melodramatic
- soft and intimate
- progressive emotional build

## Possible Genre Framework
Acoustic memorial ballad with soft piano, strings, and restrained percussion

## Vocal Direction
Tender female vocal, intimate and close, maturing emotionally as the song progresses

## Instrumentation Hints
- acoustic guitar
- soft piano
- cello
- gentle strings
- light percussion late in the song

## Special Constraints
- avoid generic military clichés
- avoid vague grief language
- use lived details
- preserve sister perspective
- make the final turn hit hard without becoming theatrical
