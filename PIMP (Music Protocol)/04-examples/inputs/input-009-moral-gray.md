# PIMP Activation

Song called "Right Thing, Wrong Time"

Two people did something they shouldn’t have, but it’s not cleanly wrong either. Timing, circumstance, other people involved.

Nobody is innocent, but nobody is fully guilty either.

## What I do want
- moral ambiguity
- no clear side
- no judgment tone
- feels unresolved

## Maybe Genre? I don't know
americana or stripped country ballad

## Random notes
- late-night drive
- hands on the steering wheel too tight
- phone lighting up but ignored
- both knew better

## Please don't do this
- no clear “we were wrong” or “we were right”
- no lesson
- no resolution
