# Example Input 002 - The Whistler

## Title
The Whistler: Outlaw Gunman

## Raw User Intent
PIMP activate on a dark Americana folk song mixed with 50s country called “The Whistler: Outlaw Gunman.” A dark violent tale of retribution and revenge. It should start and end with whistling.

## Desired Direction
- dark
- cinematic
- menacing
- dusty
- cold-blooded
- story-driven

## Possible Genre Framework
Dark Americana folk country with old-country influence and outlaw ballad energy

## Vocal Direction
Low, weathered male vocal with calm menace and narrative control

## Instrumentation Hints
- acoustic guitar
- tremolo guitar
- upright bass
- sparse snare
- harmonica or whistle motif
- possible baritone guitar

## Special Constraints
- start with whistling
- end with whistling
- avoid cartoon outlaw clichés
- avoid overexplaining the revenge
- keep language sharp and grounded
- style prompt should remain under 1000 characters
