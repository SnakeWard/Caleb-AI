# Example Output 001 - Baby Brother

## Title
Baby Brother

## Concept Summary
A soft, intimate memorial ballad told from the perspective of an older sister reflecting on childhood mischief, teenage trouble, and the unbearable finality of losing her brother to war. The identity is tender and personal, the dominant tension is love colliding with irreversible loss, and the release comes as quiet devastation rather than dramatic catharsis.

## Signal Stack Summary

### Identity
Older sister narrator; nurturing, reflective, emotionally credible, deeply personal

### Emotional Arc
Warm memory -> playful youth -> worried affection -> dread -> irreversible grief

### Genre Framework
Acoustic memorial ballad with folk-country intimacy and restrained cinematic sorrow

### Production Layer
Soft acoustic guitar, close piano, low cello, restrained strings, delicate dynamics, intimate vocal presence

## Lyrics

[Intro]
(soft acoustic guitar, faint room tone, close-mic vocal)
You came in hollerin', red-faced and wild
Mama said, “Lord, that one's gonna test us awhile”

[Verse 1]
I was a few years older, thought I knew it all
You were barefoot in the kitchen, flyin' down the hall
Throwin' fits and toy cars, slammin' every door
Then laughin' five minutes later on the living room floor

[Verse 2]
We had scraped-up knees and flashlight nights
Cardboard crowns and backyard fights
Giggles in the dark while the whole house slept
I still see the blanket forts that we never kept

[Pre-Chorus]
Then you got older, and the world got loud
Girls in your passenger seat, stereo up proud
Blue lights in the rearview, your name back at home
Lord, I spent half those years waitin' by the phone

[Chorus]
Baby brother, you were trouble and light
Half torn-up blue jeans, half heart of gold at night
You made us mad, made us laugh, made this house alive
Now I talk to your picture just to get through the night

[Verse 3]
Then came boots, short hair, and a plane headin' east
Folded shirts in a duffel where your old ball cap used to be
You said, “I'll be fine,” like boys always do
Like a sister's fear ain't a thing that can come true

[Bridge]
(low cello enters, vocal more fragile)
They brought you home in polished wood
Flag laid tight where your shoulders should've stood
Twenty-one shots cut holes through the sky
And I learned a heart can keep beatin' while somethin' in it dies

[Final Chorus]
Baby brother, you were trouble and light
A screen door slam and a porch lamp in the night
You made us mad, made us laugh, made this house alive
Now I say your name so some part of you survives

[Outro]
(soft piano, near-whisper vocal)
Your boots ain't moved
Mama still leaves that light on
