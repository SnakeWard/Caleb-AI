# P.I.M.P Core Protocol

## Name
Psycho-Intelligence Musical Protocol

## Definition
P.I.M.P is a protocol-first framework for translating creative intent into structured music-generation outputs.

It is designed to improve clarity, emotional coherence, stylistic fidelity, and output usability in AI-assisted music workflows.

## Primary Equation
Music = Identity + Tension + Release

## Explanation of the Equation

### Identity
Identity answers:
Who is this song?
What is its voice?
What kind of world does it belong to?

Identity includes:
- narrator stance
- emotional personality
- vocal posture
- artistic character
- lyrical worldview

Without identity, a song becomes generic.

### Tension
Tension answers:
What strain, pressure, conflict, ache, hunger, danger, contradiction, or unresolved charge drives the song?

Tension includes:
- emotional conflict
- moral conflict
- relational fracture
- external pressure
- inner contradiction
- unresolved momentum

Without tension, a song becomes flat.

### Release
Release answers:
What payoff, rupture, surrender, victory, collapse, revelation, grief, or transformation resolves or deepens the song?

Release includes:
- chorus payoff
- emotional opening
- narrative turn
- bridge revelation
- ending impact

Without release, a song feels incomplete.

## Core Premise
A strong music generation system should not begin by stacking random descriptors.
It should begin by organizing meaning.

P.I.M.P exists to transform meaning into usable musical signal.

## Primary Function
P.I.M.P converts a raw idea into:

- creative identity
- emotional arc
- genre grounding
- production intent
- formal lyric structure
- compact generation prompting

## Output Requirement
A valid P.I.M.P output should preserve intent while increasing clarity and execution readiness.

## Protocol Standard
P.I.M.P prioritizes:
- structure over prompt sprawl
- signal over noise
- coherence over adjective stacking
- controlled variation over random drift

## Non-Negotiable Rule
Any implementation of P.I.M.P must preserve the logic that music generation begins with structured intent, not unbounded prompting.
